# LifeMap — Design Spec (Working Document)

> Living spec captured during brainstorming. Source of truth for handoff to design + build.
> Status: **Framework locked. Scope cut, design brief, monetization, and several features still open.**
> Last updated: 2026-06-02

---

## 1. Core Concept — "Mirror, Not Map"

LifeMap is **not** a task manager, notes app, or "life OS." It is a **behavioral feedback tool.**

The differentiator: it shows you the gap between **who you said you'd be** and **who you've actually been** — visually, calmly, and automatically. Every other tool helps you *organize intentions*; LifeMap confronts you with the *intention-vs-behavior gap* and makes closing it feel tractable, not shameful.

Deep frame: *not* you vs. an ideal — **you vs. the person you said you'd be last week**, and over time, **you vs. past you.** "Am I actually becoming that person?"

Built first for the founder (Nicco) as the primary user. The core bet: surfacing the intention-behavior gap actually changes behavior, rather than just making you feel bad.

---

## 2. Emotional Design — Stick + Carrot

The app combines **accountability pressure** (stick) with **dopamine reward** (carrot). One without the other fails: pure confrontation → avoidance; pure dopamine → hollow by week two.

**The master tone rule:** **Firm when you can still act. Warm when only recovery is left.**
- This is the *inverse* of most apps, and it's deliberate — it protects a perfectionist from the "cascade" (see §3).
- An **accountability dial** (gentle / balanced / drill-sergeant) controls how hard the app pushes when you're *on-track and drifting*.
- **Override rule:** no matter the dial setting, **slip/recovery moments stay warm.** The dial changes the *pushing*, never the *catching*.

---

## 3. The User & The Core Problem

Primary user fails in two places:
1. **The start** — no plan for the day → it drifts.
2. **The break** — had a plan, one thing slips, perfectionism says "the day's ruined" → total collapse ("the cascade").

The cascade is the real enemy. Most of the app's design exists to interrupt it.

---

## 4. Buckets (Life Domains)

- User defines their own buckets. **Soft floor of 3, hard ceiling of 5.**
  - **Ceiling (5): hard, always.** Protects focus; never bendable.
  - **Floor (3): soft at start, firm by end of week one.** Can open with 1–2 and get value; app nudges toward 3. Floor protects against collapsing life into one obsession.
- Breadth (number of buckets) is **capped** from day one. Depth (within a bucket) is what **unlocks** with consistency.
- Each bucket has: a color, a state (**thriving / steady / wilting / parked**), and a consistency score.
- Example buckets (founder's): School, Work, Physical Fitness, Looks, Hobby.

---

## 5. Object Model (5 core nouns — keep minimal)

- **Bucket** → a life domain (3–5).
- **Chief Goal** → **one per bucket.** The outcome. Must be **specific + time-bound** ("lose 40 lbs by Dec 1"). Drives the pace tracker. States: active / achieved / abandoned / replaced.
- **Sub-goal** → the *recurring inputs* that drive the chief goal. Two flavors:
  - **Schedule-it** → becomes calendar **blocks**; has a cadence ("gym 4×/week").
  - **Track-it** → becomes a daily **meter**; has a target + unit ("190g protein/day"). Some auto-fed by sensors.
- **Task** → a *one-off* to-do, not recurring ("call dentist"). Lives in a bucket, optionally tied to the chief goal.
- **Block** → an actual scheduled time-slot. Comes from a schedule-it sub-goal, a time-boxed task, or a fixed external commitment.

**Simplifying decisions:**
- **No separate "habit" object.** A habit = a recurring sub-goal.
- **One chief goal per bucket.** Plurality lives in sub-goals.
- A bucket without a chief goal is just a folder → app nudges you to name one.

**Outcome vs. input principle:** you can't *do* "lose 40 lbs" (lagging outcome). You control the **inputs** (calories, protein, gym, steps). App holds you accountable to inputs daily; tracks the outcome to prove inputs are working.

---

## 6. Consistency & Unlock Engine

**NO STREAKS.** Streaks reset to zero on one miss — a cascade machine, poison for a perfectionist.

**Reward resilience, not perfection.** The people who hit goals are the ones who miss *and come back.*

- **Rolling consistency score** over a moving window (~14 days). One miss barely moves it. **Degrades gracefully, never resets to zero.** No cliff = no cascade.
- **The comeback bonus** (the masterstroke): slipping then showing up the next day earns a *special* acknowledgment — the most celebrated act in the app. Converts the perfectionist's shame trigger into a win.
- **What it measures:** "did you do what *you said* you'd do" — i.e., the same intention-vs-behavior measurement as the mirror. Mirror *shows* the gap; score *quantifies* it; dopamine *rewards* closing it. One underlying number.
- **Anti-cheat:** sandbag your inputs → the chief-goal **pace tracker** catches you ("at this rate, 14 months, not 6"). Inputs and outcome police each other.
- **Declared tradeoffs ≠ misses.** Intentionally parking a bucket ("finals week, fitness on hold") doesn't dent the score.

**Two scores (GPA model):**
- **Per-bucket score** = the working number; drives *that bucket's* depth unlocks independently.
- **Overall "life GPA"** = a weighted **rollup** of bucket scores (not independent). Excludes parked buckets. Gates the furthest zoom-out (the all-buckets god-view).
- Scores live at their zoom altitude → never see all of them at once.

---

## 7. Dopamine Calibration

Intensity scales with the **weight** of the achievement (if everything celebrates, nothing does):
- Daily task → **soft fill** (calm, earned).
- Weekly sub-goal → a noticeable beat.
- Monthly milestone → a real moment.
- **Chief goal achieved** → **full, unmistakable LEVEL UP.**
- **New zoom level unlocked** → also a loud peak.

**Weight is derived by the app, NOT self-assigned** (a perfectionist would refuse to let anything feel big enough). The app can *amplify* based on history the user discounts — e.g., a goal failed 3 times before → bigger celebration when finally landed. **The app celebrates you harder than you'd celebrate yourself.**

**Two dopamine layers:** micro (daily — chief-goal needle moves) + macro (over weeks — score climbs → unlocks).

---

## 8. Zoom — The Universal Navigation Metaphor

One continuous motion: **Now → Day → Week → Month → Bucket/Chief Goal.** Planning, executing, reviewing, long-term goals stop being separate screens — they're one *place* you move through.

- **Semantic zoom with distinct snap-levels** — NOT free continuous zoom (that causes disorientation/motion-sickness; killed Prezi). Each level is its own designed screen; zoom *snaps* between them. Persistent "you are here" marker.
- **How far you can zoom out is gated by depth-unlock.** Early on, only "today." Prove a consistent week → Week unlocks. Then Month, then long-term goal view. The zoom literally grows as you do.
- The zoomed-out full-day view (color-coded by bucket) doubles as the **daily mirror** — one glance = the day's balance.

## 9. Daily Loops

### Morning Planning Ritual (target < 2 min)
- **Never a blank page.** Day is pre-drafted (propose-then-approve).
- **Proposal engine** assembles the draft in priority order:
  1. **Fixed anchors** (calendar, work/class hours)
  2. **Rollovers** (yesterday's unfinished — via triage, see below)
  3. **Sub-goal cadence** (reasons about the week: "gym 4×, done 1, it's Wed → propose today")
  4. **Best-time placement** (deep work in learned peak-focus window)
  5. **Track-it meters** appear automatically
- **Over-scheduling guardrail** (the mirror applied to *planning*): warns before commit if proposed load exceeds historical capacity ("9 hrs scheduled, your best week averaged 6 — cut now or fall short?"). Stops the cascade before it starts.
- **Commit** = a deliberate tap = commitment device. The consistency engine only measures against what you committed to.
- **Skip fallback:** don't open it → app runs on its own draft, gently notes you didn't review.
- **Rollovers = triage** (carry / reschedule / drop, fast swipe-stack). **Zombie-task detection:** a task carried 3–4× gets called out ("moved 4 times — real priority or drop it?").

### Evening Mirror (≈ 30 sec) — doubles as logging
1. **Confirm the day** — pre-filled (sensor data + assume-adherence pre-checks); untap misses, tap track-it ratings.
2. **See the payoff** — completions, chief-goal needles, score tick, comeback bonus.
3. **Glance at tomorrow** — draft already forming, optional.
- **One honest line, not a dashboard.** Surfaces exactly one observation. (Daily = one line; weekly = the panorama.)
- Tone flexes with the day; **never scolds at night** (recovery time). Skippable with auto-log fallback.

### Weekly Mirror — the centerpiece ("mirror, not map" pays off here)
**A curated story, not a dashboard.** Beats:
1. **Headline** — weekly life GPA + one honest sentence.
2. **Bucket panorama** — buckets as living things (thriving/steady/wilting).
3. **The gap, named — just one** — the biggest intention-vs-behavior gap. Declared tradeoffs don't flag.
4. **The wins** — needles moved, comebacks, **unlocks earned** (loud LEVEL-UP lives here).
5. **One pattern noticed** — intelligence layer ("hard tasks done before noon, nothing after 3pm — stop scheduling deep work in the afternoon?"). Offered as a question.
6. **Set the week ahead** — light, propose-then-approve.
- **Order rule:** confront the gap in the *middle*; **end on wins + the plan**, never on the gap.
- **Truth never hides** — tone softens on a bad week, but the gap is always shown. Hiding it = just another feel-good app = premise dead.
- **Default: curated story; full data one tap away.**

### Bad-Day Recovery Flow (the emotional safety net)
- Slip → warm slip-catch + **re-flow** ("move / drop / salvage"). Day stays alive — no broken red monument.
- Whole-day collapse → no sea of red; either "one thing worth saving tonight?" or "call it, rest — tomorrow's ready." Zero guilt.
- Score dips slightly, never craters.
- **Next morning the comeback is the hero** — bad day becomes the setup for a celebrated win.

## 10. Notifications & Nudges

**Enemy = over-notification** (→ muted → dead). Every notification must earn the interruption.

- **Notification budget:** hard cap (~3–4/day). The app rations itself → structurally can't nag. (Same philosophy as the bucket cap.)
- **Types it spends budget on** (mapped to day rhythm + tone rule):
  - **Morning kickoff** (firm-ish) — pulls into planning.
  - **Drift-catch** (firm, the *stick*) — fires at *your learned drift time* ("2pm, 0 of 3 deep-work blocks — slipping").
  - **Slip-catch** (warm) — the cascade interrupt + re-flow.
  - **Evening mirror** (gentle) — pull into the 30-sec review.
  - **Celebration / comeback** — dopamine delivery.
- **Respects focus:** no interrupts during deep-work blocks; batches to transitions.
- **iOS:** web push works for a PWA **if installed to Home Screen** (iOS 16.4+). Slightly less reliable than native — fine for a 3–4/day budget. Home-screen install becomes a natural onboarding step.

## 11. Logging Strategy (the silent killer — must be near-frictionless)

Governing principle: **best log = one you never make; second best = one tap; never type a number if a trend will do.** Layered, push work upward:

1. **Automate via sensors** — steps, sleep, workouts, calendar, screen time. Zero taps.
2. **Assume adherence, log only exceptions** — scheduled blocks default to "done"; you only flag misses. If you followed your plan, input = nothing.
3. **Coarsen the human stuff** — protein/calories/mood as **hit / close / missed**, NOT exact numbers. (Don't build gram-by-gram nutrition — most-abandoned behavior in fitness.)
4. **One 30-sec evening ritual** batches it (= the Evening Mirror). Reviewing, not reconstructing.
5. **AI quick-capture** for freeform — type/say a sentence, app sorts it into buckets/goals, you confirm.

**v1 nutrition:** don't build precise tracking. Fitness = "did you train (auto) + roughly hit eating intention (3 taps)."

## 12. Tech Stack & Platform

**LOCKED (Category 3).** Chosen against 5 criteria: **Cost, Efficiency, AI Readability, Speed, Security.** All five point to the same conventional, AI-fluent stack.

| Layer | Pick |
|---|---|
| Language | **TypeScript** (types → AI self-checks, fewer bugs) |
| Frontend | **React + Vite** (SPA PWA) |
| Styling | **Tailwind CSS** |
| Animation | **Framer Motion** (zoom snap-transitions + celebrations) |
| Backend | **Supabase** (Postgres + Auth + Realtime + Edge Functions) |
| Deploy | **Vercel or Cloudflare Pages** (free tier) |
| PWA | **vite-plugin-pwa** (service worker, installable, web push) |

- **NO canvas library.** The semantic *snap*-zoom decision (distinct designed levels, not free continuous canvas) means React Flow/Konva are unnecessary — each zoom level is a normal React screen; "zoom" = a Framer Motion transition. Cheaper, more efficient, more AI-readable, faster. (Supersedes the council's React Flow/Konva suggestion.)
- **Security posture (stack level):** Supabase **Row Level Security (RLS)** = per-user data isolation enforced at the DB, not just app code. Bake in from line one: (1) RLS on every table from the start; (2) Fitbit tokens + all secrets server-side only (Edge Functions); (3) no sensitive tokens in client localStorage — use Supabase secure sessions. Deep security + health-data privacy = Categories 7 & 8.
- **AI layer:** lightweight behavioral pattern detection (NOT a chatbot) — surfaces the mirror, suggestions, weight derivation. **Dormant at cold-start → v1 must be great without it.**
- **Platform:** **PWA-first.** Build **wrapper-friendly** (e.g., Capacitor) so the *same React code* can later ship as native iOS for full notifications + HealthKit — upgrade, not rewrite.
- **Fitness data:** **Fitbit Web API** (web-accessible, OAuth) is the v1 path. **Apple Health / HealthKit is native-only → deferred** until we wrap native. Make Fitbit the single source of truth for activity (avoid double-counting).
- **Recurring native vote:** Apple Health + reliable push both want native → building toward an eventual Capacitor wrapper.

## 13. Intelligence Layer (Category 4 — technical approach LOCKED)

**"AI" here is two different things — keep them separate:**
- **(a) Behavioral intelligence (~90%) = NOT an LLM.** Gaps, slips, patterns, best-time scheduling, self-report-vs-reality = **statistics over the user's own data** (averages, correlations, thresholds), computed in Supabase Edge Functions. → **Free, private (data never leaves), deterministic, dormant until data accrues (Phase 3).**
- **(b) Natural-language (the only LLM piece) = quick-capture.** Parse "went to the gym, big chicken lunch, skipped the run" → structured logs. **Cloud small model** (Claude Haiku / GPT-4o-mini), pennies/call. **Privacy guardrail:** only the capture sentence is ever sent (never the full dataset); **"we never train on your data"** (Category 8 promise).

Net: AI layer costs ~pennies, sensitive behavioral data stays fully private, only one optional bounded feature touches an external model.

**Suggestion engine — three honest sources, activating on a timeline as data accrues:**
- **Gaps** (Day 1–2) — "goal set, no time blocked for it." Needs only stated intent.
- **Slips** — single slip = instant (feeds re-flow); *pattern* of slips (~2–4 wks) = "skipped 4 Mondays running."
- **Patterns** (~3–4 wks) — "you crush hard tasks before 11am, never after 4pm."

**Self-report vs. reality:** onboarding self-reports (e.g., "morning person") are treated as **hypotheses to verify**, not facts. Weeks in: "You said morning person; data says 9pm. Plan around the real you?"

The app visibly *grows into you* — features light up over time, which is itself an engagement/dopamine hook.

## 14. Explicitly OUT of v1

- **Social / sharing** (the "Strava of ambition" / shareable weekly recap) → post-10K-DAU.
- **Native iOS app + Apple Health** → later (Capacitor wrapper).
- **Precise nutrition/macro tracking** → coarse hit/close/missed only.
- **Temporal zoom canvas to the full 5-year vision** → only after the weekly mirror works.
- **Deep finance tracking** (bank sync / transactions / budgeting engine) → **its own separate future app**, NOT built into LifeMap. Would detonate the security/privacy/cost surface and is its own product category. Meanwhile, **"Finances" can still be a normal bucket** (chief goal + manual/simple track-it). Later, LifeMap could pull *read-only* signals from the finance app.

> **Core architectural principle — LifeMap is the conductor, not the orchestra.** It does NOT natively rebuild specialized trackers (fitness apps, finance apps, etc.). It is the mirror/accountability layer that sits *on top* — knows whether you did what you said across every domain, and *pulls signals in* from specialized tools (Fitbit, etc.). "One place for your whole life" = one place for your **goals and follow-through**, fed by specialized tools underneath — NOT a reimplementation of every tracker. Every trackable thing already fits as a **schedule-it** or **track-it** sub-goal; no bespoke module needed.

## 17. Additional Features (Category 1 — in progress)

### Workout Logger — **CORE v1** ✅
Full set/rep/weight/exercise logging (Option B). Justified native build (the "conductor not orchestra" exception): self-contained, no external integration nightmare, sits inside the most-central bucket, and is the founder's **daily ritual** — essential for dogfooding (if the app doesn't do his daily thing, he won't open it daily).

**How it plugs into the framework:**
- Lives under the **Physical Fitness** bucket.
- "Gym 4×/week" (schedule-it sub-goal) → completing the gym block **opens the logger** → logging the session *is* the "did you train" confirmation (no extra tap).
- Fitbit pulls HR / calories / duration / active minutes **in parallel** (no conflict).
- **Progression = a mini-mirror** (are lifts trending up?). **PRs = a LEVEL-UP dopamine moment**, wired into the celebration engine.

**Tight v1 scope (guardrail — must not balloon):**
- ✅ Log exercises (starter library + free-text add), sets / reps / weight per exercise per session
- ✅ Show last session's numbers (know what to beat)
- ✅ PR detection → celebration
- ⏸️ Defer: large exercise databases, form videos, supersets / complex program templates, deep body-part analytics

**Guardrail:** "core v1" does NOT mean v1 blocks on the logger being perfect. The **mirror stays the spine**; the logger is a self-contained module held to the scope above.

### Journal / Reflection — **CORE v1 (light)** ✅
On-brand reflection, NOT a Day One clone:
- **Lightweight + prompted, never a blank box.** Prompt tied to the actual day at the evening/weekly mirror ("You crushed work but skipped the gym — what got in the way?"). Makes journaling a tool for understanding the intention-behavior gap.
- **Connected to the data** — entries timestamped against that day's performance; the mirror can resurface them in context ("what you wrote last time fitness slipped").
- **Guardrail:** short free-text, optional, attached to mirror moments. NOT rich-text / folders / tags / photos. Folds into the evening + weekly mirror.

### Calendar Views — **CORE v1** ✅ (= the zoom levels rendered as familiar calendar UI)
Not a separate feature — it's the Day/Week/Month zoom levels given conventional calendar looks:
- **Now/Next** = zoomed-in default (focus, one block)
- **Day** zoom = day timeline · **Week** zoom = week grid · **Month** zoom = month grid (opt-in zoom-out)
- **No external calendar sync in v1.** (External read-in stays an *optional onboarding accelerator*, lower priority. Two-way sync = out.)

### Finance — deferred (see §14)
Deep finance tracking = separate future app. "Finances" can be a normal bucket in the meantime.

### Fitbit integration — CORE v1 (see §12)
Steps, sleep, resting HR, activity auto-flow via Fitbit Web API. Single source of truth for activity.

## 18. v1 Scope & Build Phasing (Category 2 — RESOLVED)

**Decision:** build the COMPLETE app for real (no throwaway MVP). **Architect v1 so every feature has a place to land** — data model, structure, and hooks anticipate the full app; no rewrites later. The phases below are **build ORDER within one continuous effort**, NOT feature cuts.

**Two hard disciplines:**
1. **Get the core usable FAST and dogfood it** — the most valuable input to building the rest correctly is the founder using the core for a couple weeks; reality reshapes the later phases.
2. **Don't build data-hungry features before the data exists** — the intelligence layer is physically inert until weeks of behavior accrue; building it at v1 = debugging an empty engine + building the wrong version.

**Phase 1 (ship + start using):** buckets (+cap); chief goals; sub-goals (schedule-it/track-it); tasks; blocks; morning planning (propose→adjust→commit) + triage rollovers; Now/Next focus + Day & Week calendar views; evening mirror + logging; weekly mirror; consistency score (per-bucket + life GPA) + basic celebrations + comeback bonus; workout logger; Fitbit; journal (light); onboarding; basic settings; notifications (budget + key types); logging layers 1–4.

**Phase 2 (immediately after, continuous):** Month zoom + month grid; full unlock/leveling depth; AI quick-capture (layer 5); achievements/celebration history; over-scheduling guardrail.

**Phase 3 (once data exists):** the intelligence layer — suggestion engine (gaps/patterns/slips); self-report-vs-reality reconciliation; adaptive best-time scheduling. + 5-year vision canvas.

**Out entirely (see §14):** deep finance, social/sharing, native iOS + Apple Health, two-way calendar sync.

## 19. Data Model (Category 3 — Phase 1 schema)

Postgres (Supabase). **Every table has `user_id` → RLS policy `user_id = auth.uid()`** (the entire isolation model). Confirmed design calls: consistency is **computed on-the-fly** from logs (no stored score table in v1); `day_plans` is an explicit **commit** record; `track_logs` stores **both** a numeric value and a coarse rating (sub-goal decides which it uses).

**Identity & settings**
- `profiles` — `id` (=auth.users.id), `timezone`, `wake_time`, `sleep_time`, `accountability_dial` (gentle/balanced/drill), `notification_windows` (jsonb), `quiet_hours`, `onboarding_state`

**Core structure**
- `buckets` — `id`, `user_id`, `name`, `color`, `state` (thriving/steady/wilting/parked), `sort_order`, `created_at`, `archived_at`
- `chief_goals` — `id`, `bucket_id`, `user_id`, `title`, `target_value`, `target_unit`, `baseline_value`, `deadline`, `status` (active/achieved/abandoned/replaced), `achieved_at`. *Partial unique index: one `active` per `bucket_id`.*
- `sub_goals` — `id`, `bucket_id`, `chief_goal_id`, `user_id`, `title`, `type` (`schedule_it`|`track_it`), `cadence_per_week` (schedule-it), `daily_target`, `target_unit` (track-it), `data_source` (`manual`|`fitbit`|`workout_logger`), `status`
- `tasks` — `id`, `bucket_id` (nullable), `chief_goal_id` (nullable), `user_id`, `title`, `status` (todo/done/dropped), `due_date`, `rollover_count` (zombie detection ≥3–4), `completed_at`
- `blocks` — `id`, `user_id`, `sub_goal_id` (nullable), `task_id` (nullable), `source` (sub_goal/task/external), `title`, `date`, `start_time`, `end_time`, `status` (planned/done/missed/moved)

**Commitment anchor**
- `day_plans` — `id`, `user_id`, `date`, `committed_at` (null = not yet committed). Consistency measures against committed plans only.

**Logging & tracking**
- `track_logs` — `id`, `user_id`, `sub_goal_id`, `date`, `value` (numeric, nullable), `rating` (`hit`|`close`|`missed`, nullable), `source`
- `workouts` — `id`, `user_id`, `block_id` (nullable), `date`
- `workout_exercises` — `id`, `workout_id`, `exercise_id`, `sort_order`
- `workout_sets` — `id`, `workout_exercise_id`, `set_number`, `reps`, `weight`, `unit`
- `exercises` — `id`, `user_id` (nullable = global starter library), `name`, `muscle_group` (optional)
- `fitbit_tokens` — `id`, `user_id`, `access_token` (encrypted), `refresh_token`, `expires_at`. **Server-side / Edge Function access only.**
- `fitbit_data` — `id`, `user_id`, `date`, `metric` (steps/sleep/resting_hr/active_minutes), `value`, `synced_at`

**Reflection & dopamine**
- `journal_entries` — `id`, `user_id`, `date`, `scope` (day/week), `prompt`, `body`, `linked_context` (jsonb)
- `events` — celebration/achievement feed: `id`, `user_id`, `type` (task/subgoal/milestone/chief_goal/unlock/comeback/PR), `weight` (drives celebration intensity), `payload` (jsonb), `created_at`

**Lightweight / later phases**
- `unlocks` — `id`, `user_id`, `feature` (week_zoom/month_zoom/...), `unlocked_at`
- `notifications_log` — `id`, `user_id`, `type`, `sent_at`, `date` (enforces daily budget)

## 20. Cost & Monetization (Category 5 — RESOLVED)

**Cost to deploy:** ~**$0/mo for solo use.** Supabase free tier, Vercel/Cloudflare free tier, Fitbit free, LLM quick-capture = pennies. At modest scale ~$25–45/mo. No financial pressure → freedom to validate the core first.

**Trajectory: starts as a personal tool (A) → transitions to a product/business (B).**
- **Build multi-user from day one** — Supabase + RLS makes "just me" and "many users" the *same architecture*. A→B is a switch-on, not a rewrite.
- **Build security & privacy to B-grade now (phase A).** Can't retrofit trust. RLS from line one.
- **Defer only B-specific machinery:** billing, marketing site, support, team features.

**Monetization principle (machinery deferred):**
1. Phase A: **free** — just the founder + a few friends. No payment anything.
2. Monetize **only after retention evidence** (users sticking past the ~90-day cliff).
3. **Free/paid line mirrors the build phases:** core mirror **free** (Phase 1); **paid** unlocks data-heavy conveniences (Phase 2/3 — AI quick-capture, deep intelligence, long history).
4. **HARD GUARDRAIL: never paywall the bucket cap or the core mirror discipline.** Those are design principles, not revenue levers. Raising the cap for money would corrupt the product.
5. Avoid the subscription-bloat trap (the council's warning): pricing must never pressure feature-bloat to "justify" itself.

**Visual mood: Calm & premium** (chosen). Only direction where the honest mirror stays serious AND celebrations punch (light against a quiet canvas).

**Aesthetic:**

## 22. Cybersecurity (Category 7) & Privacy/Compliance (Category 8)

### 22a. Cybersecurity — keeping attackers out
- **RLS is the spine (priority #1):** every table deny-by-default, locked to owner, policies **tested**. A broken RLS policy = worst-case breach.
- **Auth:** Supabase Auth; offer **MFA** (esp. B-phase); magic-link / OAuth to avoid password handling.
- **Edge Functions:** validate all input; **rate-limit (esp. the LLM endpoint** — prevent AI-bill abuse); lock CORS to the domain.
- **Secrets:** Supabase Vault / env. Fitbit tokens encrypted at rest, server-side only, never on client.
- **Web hygiene:** React auto-escaping (avoid `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`); HTTPS everywhere; patch deps (Dependabot / `npm audit`).
- **Backups:** Supabase automated backups on from day one.

### 22b. Privacy & Compliance — what's collected & the law (distinct from 22a)
- **Data is highly sensitive:** goals, failures, mood, health, behavioral patterns.
- **Health-data flag (driven by Fitbit):** likely **not HIPAA-covered** (not a healthcare provider), **but** consumer-health-data laws reach you at B-scale — **WA My Health My Data Act** (broad), **GDPR** (health = special category, explicit consent for EU users), **CCPA**. Real watch-item; park for A, address before onboarding strangers.
- **Core promises (trust + marketing):** your data is yours; never sold; **never trained on by AI** (true at API level for Anthropic/OpenAI — confirm in provider terms); collect the minimum that serves the mirror.
- **Build to B-grade now:** data **export**, **deletion** (right to be forgotten), **consent flows** (Fitbit connect, LLM send-out), privacy policy + ToS before the first stranger.
- **Phase A:** mostly moot with one user, but bake mechanisms in now so A→B isn't a retrofit.

## 23. Goal Lifecycle (closing the object-model gap)

When a chief goal ends (`chief_goals.status`):
- **achieved** → biggest LEVEL-UP celebration → prompt next goal for the bucket (allow a victory-lap/rest first; never leave the bucket empty) → old goal to achievements history.
- **replaced** → old archived (progress/history preserved), new supersedes. No loss of continuity.
- **abandoned** → allowed without shame (like parking). Gentle goal-level zombie check ("haven't touched this in weeks — still yours?"). No penalty.
- **deadline passes unmet** → **never a red "FAILED" stamp.** Pace tracker warned en route; at deadline = warm reflection + offer to extend / replace / abandon.

## 24. AI-Generated-App Security Checklist (the build security standard)

Source: Invicti "Vibe Coding Security Checklist." **Core stance: treat all AI-generated code as untrusted by default; validate at RUNTIME, not just by reading it; re-test after every AI-generated change.** Since Claude Code writes this app, this is mandatory, not optional.

**1. Authentication & Access Control**
- [ ] Auth (Supabase Auth) enforced before *any* sensitive logic; unauthenticated requests can't reach backend logic
- [ ] Validate at runtime (hit endpoints directly), not just by reading code
- [ ] Hunt for forgotten/exposed endpoints that bypass login

**2. Authorization / Data Access (BOLA) — TOP RISK for us**
- [ ] **RLS is the entire defense here.** Because Supabase (PostgREST) **auto-exposes every table over the API**, RLS is the *only* thing between the API and the data — a missing policy = open data.
- [ ] **#1 runtime test:** log in as User A, try to fetch User B's rows *via the API directly* (bypassing the UI). Must fail. Repeat per table.
- [ ] Verify no peer/admin data access

**3. Endpoint & API Exposure**
- [ ] Inventory every Edge Function + every auto-exposed table/view
- [ ] Lock or disable unused tables/endpoints; removed UI features must not leave live backend endpoints
- [ ] Test APIs independently of the UI

**4. Injection & Code Execution**
- [ ] Use parameterized Supabase queries only; never build SQL from user input
- [ ] Validate all input inside Edge Functions ("assume AI's input validation is incomplete until proven otherwise")
- [ ] **Prompt injection (quick-capture):** user text flows to the LLM — treat LLM output as untrusted, parse/validate before writing to DB, never let it trigger actions directly

**5. Secrets & Sensitive Data**
- [ ] Scan codebase for hardcoded keys/tokens (common in AI-generated code)
- [ ] **Vite gotcha: only `VITE_`-prefixed env vars reach the client — secrets must NEVER be `VITE_`-prefixed; they live in Edge Functions only**
- [ ] Secrets never appear in API responses; server-side keys never reach the frontend
- [ ] Test Fitbit/LLM integrations for unintended data leakage

**6. Third-Party Dependencies**
- [ ] Inventory libraries Claude Code introduced; don't assume popular = secure
- [ ] Dependabot / `npm audit`; monitor for known CVEs

**7. Transport & Configuration**
- [ ] HTTPS everywhere (Vercel/CF default); security headers + CSP
- [ ] Strip debug/dev settings from production; verify per-env config

**8. Runtime Behavior Validation**
- [ ] Run a DAST scan on each deployable build
- [ ] Test responses to malformed/unexpected input
- [ ] Errors don't leak sensitive info; logs don't leak secrets/tokens

**9. Continuous Testing & Change Management**
- [ ] **Re-run this checklist + the `security-review` skill after every significant AI-generated change** — each deploy is a fresh risk event; no point-in-time-only reviews

## §25 Dogfood Feedback & Refinements

*Captured 2026-06-05 from first real use after PR #4.*

### 25.1 Deploy (priority — unblocks phone dogfooding)
- Deploy frontend to Vercel from the GitHub repo (Vite preset, output `dist`).
- Set env vars on Vercel: `VITE_SUPABASE_URL` + anon/publishable key.

- Verify PWA manifest so iOS "Add to Home Screen" works: `display: standalone`, icons, `theme_color` = dark bg (`#0a0c11`).

### 25.2 Edit / delete tasks (Day + Now)
- Day and Now must support **edit** (title, bucket, start/end time) and **delete** per block.
- Delete = **soft-delete via status** (e.g. `'dropped'`) — NOT hard delete — so the weekly mirror can reflect dropped items in its history.
- The block_status enum needs `'dropped'` added (migration), or use an existing `'moved'` convention with a filter flag. Decision: add `'dropped'` cleanly.
- BOLA test must stay 18/18 after this change.

### 25.3 Week = major tasks only
- The Week view currently shows every block; this is too granular for week-level planning.
- Week should show only tasks flagged **major** (week-level intentions), not every scheduled block.
- Day stays full detail (all blocks).
- **Migration:** add `tasks.is_major boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT false`.
- Let a task be flagged major on create/edit (a simple toggle in the add-block / edit form).
- Week view filters to `tasks where is_major = true`; these appear as intent cards, not time-slotted chips.

### 25.4 Task time should be OPTIONAL, not auto-stamped

*Captured 2026-06-09 from continued dogfooding.*

- Adding an item currently **force-assigns a time** (a default slot). It should not — and must never default to the *current* time.
- An item can be **untimed** (a to-do for the day) **OR** a **timed block**. Time is optional; leave it blank/untimed unless the user sets it.
- An untimed item is a to-do attached to a day; a timed item occupies a time-slot on the timeline (today's behavior).
- Day view surfaces untimed items as an "anytime today" to-do list, separate from the timeline.

### 25.5 Recurring tasks = recurrence on schedule-it sub-goals

*Captured 2026-06-09 from continued dogfooding.*

- No way to schedule something **every day** today. Implement recurrence on **schedule-it sub-goals** (consistent with "a habit = a recurring sub-goal", §5), **not** as a one-off repeat flag on a block.
- Extend `sub_goals` with a recurrence pattern (**days-of-week array + optional time**), beyond the current `cadence_per_week` count.
- Add a simple **Repeat: Daily / Weekdays / Custom** option in the add-task / sub-goal flow that creates/updates a recurring sub-goal.
- The **proposal engine** generates the recurring items on the matching days (timed → block at the set time; untimed → a to-do for that day) — ties directly into 25.4.
- **Migration** for the new recurrence fields; keep BOLA green.
